Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful Answers for New Patients

Have questions before scheduling your visit? Here are common inquiries about our branches, consultations, and stays.

Which branch provides inpatient accommodation? +
Inpatient accommodation is available exclusively at our Thodiyoor main branch. The Thodiyoor center provides peaceful rooms, 24/7 care, physician rounds, and freshly prepared therapeutic Ayurvedic meals. Our Mynagappally branch is dedicated strictly to outpatient consultations and day therapies.
Are online video consultations available? +
Yes, Dr. Varsha offers online consultations for patients who live outside Kerala or prefer consulting from home. You can book an appointment via our website or directly on WhatsApp, and follow-up medicines can be dispatched to your location.
Does the center manufacture its own medicines? +
No. Medicines are dispensed according to Dr. Varsha's clinical prescriptions. To ensure the highest pharmacological quality and safety, our treatment center is affiliated with the respected Nagarjuna Ayurvedic Group for standard herbal preparations, oils, and decoctions.
How do I book an appointment or treatment? +
You can simply click the “Book Consultation” button on our website to open our enquiry pop-up, or message us directly on WhatsApp at +91 7012135309. Our team will promptly confirm available time slots.
