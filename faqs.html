Branches & Accommodation
Which branch offers inpatient accommodation? +
Inpatient accommodation is available strictly at our Thodiyoor main branch. The Thodiyoor center is equipped with serene, hygienic residential rooms, 24/7 care support, daily doctor rounds, classical therapy theaters, and specialized Ayurvedic meals prepared to your dietary prescription. Our Mynagappally center operates as an outpatient consultation and day therapy branch.
What amenities are included with Thodiyoor residential stays? +
Inpatient admissions at Thodiyoor include:
  • Comfortable, peaceful patient rooms ensuring deep rest and hygiene.
  • Daily doctor evaluations and monitoring of treatment responses.
  • Customized therapeutic diet (Pathya) prepared strictly according to Ayurvedic principles.
  • Assistance for accompanying caregivers / family members Details upon inquiry.
What is the difference between Thodiyoor and Mynagappally? +
Thodiyoor is our primary residential healing campus offering full inpatient Panchakarma stays as well as outpatient services. Mynagappally is an accessible outpatient clinic where patients can consult Dr. Varsha, undergo short daily therapies (like Abhyanga or Shirodhara), and collect prescribed Nagarjuna medicines.
Appointments & Online Consultations
How do I schedule a consultation with Dr. Varsha? +
You can easily request an appointment by clicking any “Book Consultation” button on our website to open the enquiry pop-up, or by messaging our reception directly on WhatsApp at +91 7012135309. Our team will verify doctor timings and confirm your slot promptly.
How do online consultations work for outstation patients? +
For patients residing outside Kerala or unable to visit physically, Dr. Varsha conducts scheduled video or voice consultations via WhatsApp. Prior to the call, you can share previous medical reports. After the consultation, a detailed treatment plan, dietary guidance, and prescriptions are provided.
Can I walk in without a prior appointment? +
While walk-in patients are accommodated subject to doctor availability, we strongly recommend booking in advance to minimize waiting time, especially if you wish to undergo therapeutic sessions on the same day.
Medicines & Affiliation
What is the center's relationship with Nagarjuna Medicines? +
Dr. Varsha's Ayurvedic Treatment Center is officially affiliated with Nagarjuna Ayurvedic Group for the supply and dispensing of standard classical formulations, oils, and herbal products. This guarantees authentic, standardized, and safe medicines for our patients.
Does the clinic manufacture its own medicines? +
No. Our clinic does not manufacture medicines in-house. All medicinal formulations dispensed at our pharmacies are sourced from authorized, quality-certified manufacturers, predominantly Nagarjuna Medicines.
Treatments & Pricing
How are treatment costs and durations determined? +
Every patient's constitution (Prakriti) and chronicity of illness are unique. While single therapy sessions have indicative fee ranges, comprehensive treatment cycles and residential packages are finalized following Dr. Varsha's clinical assessment. Personalized Consultation
