Core Clinical Principles

The Four Pillars of Our Clinical Approach

Ayurveda does not believe in a one-size-fits-all remedy. Our clinical methodology honors classical protocols adapted to the demands of contemporary lifestyles.

🌿

Root Cause (Nidana)

We identify the underlying cellular toxins (Ama) and dietary or emotional triggers creating distress, rather than treating superficial manifestations.

⚖️

Dosha Equilibrium

Restoring optimal balance between Vata (movement & nerve function), Pitta (metabolism & digestion), and Kapha (structural stability).

🥣

Pathya (Therapeutic Diet)

“Without wholesome diet, medicine is of no use. With wholesome diet, medicine is of little need.” We tailor nutritious therapeutic meals.

✨

Classical Purification

Supervised classical Panchakarma therapies (Abhyanga, Swedana, Vasti, Shirodhara) to detoxify deep tissues and boost immunity (Ojas).
